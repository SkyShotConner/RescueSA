About RescueSA

Building a better way to discover emergency services across South Africa.

Our Mission

RescueSA aims to create a central directory where people can discover emergency services, rescue organizations, hospitals and other critical services across South Africa.

What RescueSA Provides

  • Emergency service directories
  • Fire and rescue information
  • Emergency medical services
  • Air ambulance information
  • Search and rescue organizations
  • Hospital information
  • Emergency service locations

Important Information

RescueSA is an information platform and is not an emergency dispatch service. Information should always be independently verified before being relied upon during an emergency.
